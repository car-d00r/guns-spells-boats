# Fight for Princess
## Overview
Constantine, Malachi's brother is meeting up with two members of the Catholic church to hand over Princess who he received from his father. The Catholics are dopplegangers. Bartholomei, one of Danglethorp's captains and someone in contract with the demon of deception, will be there to ambush and try and steal Princess. He will be aided by a shadow demon he has command of. Peter is also there, but has been instructed to hide in the bush as to not get hurt. If he thinks he has an opportunity to steal princess he will disobey this order and try and help, much to Bart's dismay.
## Dopplegangers (Catholics)
Two dopplegangers will be waiting there with Ham slumped by them. They retrieved him from where the last doppleganger to die threw him. They heard from the doppleganger who had been impersonating Malachi's father that Ham was traveling to the island and had the same magical scent as Princess. After telling this to the Demon of deception, he was instructed to take the boy as well as Princess. The dopplegangers will be on the side of constantine at first, praising him and telling him how wonderful it is to meet the eldest son of Elias, vice chair of the holy council to the Pope. As Constantine does not give over princess right away, seeing that they have Ham and he recognizes him from camp, they will grow impatient with him and start ordering him around. As the tension between them increases, if the party does nothing, Bart will ambush them.
## Constantine Upontrent
Constantine is already feeling weird about being given this cat to hold on to and then give to a couple clerics on this weird island in the middle of nowhere. His Dad didn't give him any explanation when he dropped it off, and didn't say much else to him despite not seeing him in months (the longest it had been in some time). Then there had been that man Bartholomei who had offered to take the cat off his hands for several thousand gold pieces. They had almost come to blows when they had been interrupted by a blue boy with horns which had defused the situation. Upon seeing that very same blue boy now unconscious by the side of these clerics, his suspicion will come to a climax and he will refuse to hand over the cat with explanation of what is going on with this cat and why they hold a boy who he knows was participating in the canatum hostage now? This is when the dopplegangers will grow impatient. He will be quite shocked at the appearance of his brother whenever he enters the fray. He will try to talk to him while they are doing battle.
## Princess (Glasya)
The cage she finds herself in is magical in its ability to prevent the captive from regaining any spellslots or actions. In this cage she is rendered nothing but a cat. It is quite the insane contraption to be able to imprison an Arch-devil. It was provided to the demon of deception by Mephistopheles himself. Princess is primarily concerned with escaping. Though she will be quite surprised by the appearance of both Kragor and Ham. She may try to talk to both of them. She will mostly urge him to get away from here and get to safety. Above all else, they both cannot be captured. To Ham, she will say there is must she has to explain, but now is not the time. If she does leave before being able to say more, she will tell Ham he is her son, that she loves him, and that to trust Heimdall above anyone else, he is sworn to protect you under contract with her.
## Bartholomei Simmerstone
Bartholomei is one of Danglethorp's Captains. Peter is his Vice Captain. Peter thinks it's because his Dad wants him to learn from Bart but it's actually because his Dad trusts Bart to babysit him and keep him safe. Bart's main goal here is not to kill anyone, but to instead retrieve Princess for his master, the demon of deception. He has not been told much about why other than that the cat is not a cat but a magical beast of immense power the demon wishes to consume. This is of course not the truth. Bart is willing to kill in order to get Princess, but fighting is not his main goal. Protesting Peter, who insisted on coming along, and retrieving the cat are his two main priorities. He will have plane-shift allowing himself to teleport out of there with Peter and Princess if needed.
## Peter Teetertinkle
Peter doesn't really know what's going on. All he knows is that Bartholomei departed from the main party to do something for his father that he would not specify to the rest of the crew and disciplined them for their insolent curiosity. Peter, knowing he would not be punished. Decided to try and follow Bart. Once Bart discovered it was Peter following him, he decided it was too late to try and send bring him back himself so he was told he could come along but that he must stay hidden and get involved under no circumstances. Peter will listen initially, but spring into action the moment he thinks he can help. He may be used as leverage against Bart by the dopplegangers or whoever else.
